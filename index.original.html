Frequently Asked Roadside Questions

How fast will the tow truck reach my location? expand_more

Within Bhilwara city and bypass limits, our arrival time is typically 15 to 25 minutes. On NH-48 and district highways (Chittorgarh or Ajmer corridor), our dispatched mobile patrol typically reaches within 25 to 35 minutes depending on distance.

Can you safely tow automatic or 4x4 / AWD vehicles? expand_more

Yes. We have dedicated flatbed hydraulic carriers and wheel dollies specially engineered for automatic transmission and AWD / 4WD vehicles. This guarantees your drivetrain and gearbox suffer zero friction or wear during transit.

What if my car is overturned or stuck in a roadside ditch? expand_more

Our heavy recovery crane equipped with a 15-ton winch boom is built specifically for off-road retrieval, rollovers, divider entrapment, and deep ditch extraction without worsening body damage.

What payment modes do you accept on the spot? expand_more

We accept all UPI payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), cash, and digital net banking transfers. You receive a proper receipt for insurance reimbursement.
